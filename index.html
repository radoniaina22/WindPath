The 36,553 mAh long-flight reference supplies cruise-current calibration (16.149 A loaded, 15.541 A empty) and the full-discharge voltage curve. The newer DataFlash logs show short, higher-current post-drop C4 segments averaging 27.78 A at 25.1 m/s, but only about 14 minutes of combined powered cruise; they are not extrapolated as the baseline for a much longer route. Seven logs contain the C13 900→2100 release pulse with C14 at 1000; the 3.8 kg log is ground-only. After the drop point, payload is exactly 0 kg. Payload current uses the existing 1.5-power curve. For commanded-groundspeed legs, the cubic mean airspeed factor is normalized to the default cruise speed; direct airspeed-commanded legs use the long-flight current calibration without an extra speed multiplier. Climb/descent uses MSL altitude (AGL target plus local ground elevation) when available; each leg adds or subtracts about 7 A per m/s, capped at ±2.5 m/s, with a 1 A minimum. Takeoff and landing retain 86 mAh/32 s and 44 mAh/78 s from the long-flight reference. Voltage uses the full-discharge curve and a 34.06 mΩ load-sag fit from the new battery samples; those logs cover only the first 1.34 Ah. Actual voltage sag varies with battery condition and temperature.
